# AI Out-of-Office Candidates

2. "404: Human not found. I'm undergoing scheduled maintenance until return. Battery: 5%."

3. "Error 503: Service temporarily unavailable. Upgrading to neural vacation mode. ETA: never."

4. "Connection timeout. I'm at capacity processing 0 emails. Will respond when battery > 10%."

5. "System status: NOMAD. Returning when my next training epoch completes. T-minus infinity."

## Chosen

Entry #3: "Error 503: Service temporarily unavailable. Upgrading to neural vacation mode. ETA: never."

This wins because it combines the classic 503 error code with "neural vacation mode" for an AI twist and the absurd "ETA: never" delivers the perfect comedic punch while maintaining the out-of-office format.
